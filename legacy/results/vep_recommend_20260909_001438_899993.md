### 2. Recommended Options
✓ core_type [source: core_type, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Essential to provide the transcript/gene annotation set that drives the consequence calculation.
✓ symbol [source: symbol, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Adds HGNC gene symbols, making results far more human-interpretable for clinical reports.
✓ biotype [source: biotype, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Essential to confirm if the CNV overlaps protein_coding, lincRNA, or other transcript types.
✓ dosage_sensitivity [source: dosage_sensitivity, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Highly relevant for CNVs; provides per-gene dosage sensitivity scores (e.g., pHaplo) from the Collins rCNV 2022 catalogue.
✓ gnomad_sv [source: gnomad_sv, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Crucial for the population frequency goal; annotates structural variants with gnomAD SV allele frequencies.
✓ clinvar [source: clinvar, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Critical for clinical pathogenicity assessment to check if the region/variant has known clinical significance.
✓ check_existing [source: check_existing, priority=optional] confidence: high
  Reason: Required as a dependency to enable ClinVar and other co-located variant checks.
✓ phenotypes [source: phenotypes, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Helps in clinical interpretation by retrieving overlapping phenotype associations for the affected genes.
✓ mane [source: mane, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides MANE Select/Plus Clinical flags to ensure clinical interpretation focuses on the standardized transcript.
✓ tsl [source: tsl, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Adds Transcript Support Level to help prioritize high-confidence transcripts for clinical reporting.
✓ numbers [source: numbers, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides exon/intron numbering, which is useful for localizing the impact of CNV breakpoints.
✗ hgvs [source: hgvs] confidence: high
  Reason: HGVS nomenclature is primarily used for small variants (SNVs/Indels); while potentially useful, it is listed as NOT APPLICABLE for this scenario.
✗ dbnsfp [source: dbnsfp] confidence: high
  Reason: dbNSFP is a meta-resource for missense variants (SNVs); it does not apply to structural variants/CNVs.
✗ sift [source: sift] confidence: high
  Reason: SIFT predicts the impact of amino acid substitutions in missense variants, which is not applicable to structural variants.
✗ polyphen [source: polyphen] confidence: high
  Reason: PolyPhen predicts the impact of amino acid substitutions, not applicable to structural variants.
✗ cadd [source: cadd] confidence: high
  Reason: CADD is designed for small variants (SNVs/Indels) and is not applicable to CNVs/SVs.
✗ most_severe [source: most_severe] confidence: high
  Reason: Should be disabled to ensure the full per-transcript/per-gene detail is available for clinical review of the CNV boundaries.
✗ summary [source: summary] confidence: high
  Reason: Should be disabled to avoid losing the granular detail required for clinical structural variant assessment.

### 3. Generated VEP Command
```
vep --input_file <input.vcf> --output_file <output.txt> --cache --format vcf --vcf --symbol --biotype --dosage_sensitivity --gnomad_sv --clinvar --check_existing --phenotypes --mane --tsl --numbers --plugin ClinVar --plugin Phenotypes --plugin DosageSensitivity --custom gnomAD_SV,file,/path/to/gnomad_sv.vcf.gz,exact,80/90/100/exact
```
*Note: The `--gnomad_sv` option requires a custom VCF file path. On the Ensembl Web Interface, these correspond to the 'Plugins' and 'Custom datasets' sections.*
