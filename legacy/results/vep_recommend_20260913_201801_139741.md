  Reason: Necessary to drive the consequence calculation using the standard Ensembl/GENCODE annotation set.
✓ symbol [source: symbol, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Adds HGNC gene symbols to make results human-readable for clinical interpretation.
✓ biotype [source: biotype, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides context on whether the disrupted region belongs to a protein_coding gene or other feature.
✓ gnomad_sv [source: gnomad_sv, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Essential for structural variants to provide population allele frequencies from gnomAD-SV.
✓ clinvar [source: clinvar, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides clinical significance (e.g., pathogenic) for co-located variants, aiding pathogenicity assessment.
✓ check_existing [source: check_existing, priority=optional] confidence: high
  Reason: Required to enable the lookup of co-located variants in ClinVar and gnomAD.
✓ dosage_sensitivity [source: dosage_sensitivity, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Highly relevant for CNVs to determine if the gene is sensitive to dosage changes (e.g., haploinsufficiency).
✓ phenotypes [source: phenotypes, priority=recommended] confidence: medium
  Reason: Helps in clinical interpretation by linking disrupted genes to known phenotypes.
✗ hgvs [source: hgvs] confidence: high
  Reason: HGVS nomenclature is primarily for short variants (SNVs/indels) and is often not applicable or useful for CNVs/SVs.
✗ sift [source: sift] confidence: high
  Reason: SIFT is a missense predictor for single nucleotide substitutions; it is NOT APPLICABLE to structural variants.
✗ polyphen [source: polyphen] confidence: high
  Reason: PolyPhen is a missense predictor for single nucleotide substitutions; it is NOT APPLICABLE to structural variants.
✗ cadd [source: cadd] confidence: high
  Reason: CADD is designed for small variants (SNVs/indels) and is NOT APPLICABLE to structural variants.
✗ most_severe [source: most_severe] confidence: high
  Reason: For SV/CNV analysis, you need to see all overlapping transcripts/consequences rather than a single collapsed term.
✗ summary [source: summary] confidence: high
  Reason: A summary output would hide the granular transcript-level detail required for clinical SV interpretation.
