### 2. Recommended Options
✓ transcript_set [source: gencode_basic, priority=critical] confidence: high
  Reason: Using GENCODE Basic provides a high-quality, high-confidence subset of transcripts, which is standard for clinical/germline pipelines to reduce noise.

✓ mane_select [source: mane_select, priority=critical] confidence: high
  Reason: Essential for human GRCh38 analysis to prioritize the single most representative transcript per gene as defined by NCBI and Eanno.

✓ canonical [source: canonical, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Helps identify the primary transcript, which is critical for interpreting the biological impact of a variant.

✓ pick [source: pick, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Crucial for large datasets to avoid being overwhelmed by multiple transcripts; it selects the best representative consequence per variant.

✓ sift [source: sift, priority=critical] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides essential sequence-homology-based predictions of amino acid substitution effects.

✓ polyphen [source: polyphen, priority=critical] confidence: high
  Reason: Adds structural/functional impact predictions (Human only) to complement SIFT.

✓ cadd [source: cadd, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Integrates multiple annotations into a single score of deleteriousness, highly useful for ranking variants.

✓ alphamissense [source: alphamissense, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides state-of-the-art AI-based pathogenicity predictions for missense variants.

✓ clinvar [source: check_existing, priority=critical] confidence: high
  Reason: Directly links your variants to known clinical significance in the ClinVar database.

✓ check_existing [source: check_existing, priority=critical] confidence: high
  Reason: Essential for identifying co-located known variants and rsIDs.

✓ gnomad_af [source: gnomad_af, priority=critical] confidence: high
  Reason: Allows you to filter out common polymorphisms that are unlikely to cause rare diseases.

✓ hgvs [source: hgvs, priority=critical] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides standardized nomenclature (HGVSc/HGVSp) necessary for clinical reporting and downstream tools.

✓ symbol [source: symbol, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Replaces Ensembl IDs with human-readable gene symbols (e.g., BRCA1), making results interpretable.

✓ protein [source: protein, priority=optional] confidence: high
  Reason: Useful for linking variants to specific protein identifiers (ENSP) for structural analysis.

✓ spliceai [source: spliceai, priority=recommended] confidence: medium
  Reason: Highly recommended to detect the impact of non-coding/intronic variants on splicing, even if they are not at canonical sites.

✗ per_gene [source: per_gene] confidence: high
  Reason: Disabled in favor of `--pick`, which is more standard for variant-centric analysis using MANE Select.

✗ most_severe [source: most_severe] confidence: high
  Reason: Disabled because it conflicts with the need for detailed transcript information and identifiers (HGVS/Symbol).

✗ summary [source: summary] confidence: high
  Reason: Disabled to ensure we see per-transcript consequence details rather than a condensed list.

✗ population_genetics_specific_options [source: af_1kg, gnomad_sv] confidence: medium
  Reason: Excluded as the focus is pathogenicity/clinical relevance rather than continental population frequency shifts or SV counts.
