### 2. Recommended Options
✓ core_type [source: core_type, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Essential to use the Ensembl/GENCODE core annotation set to drive consequence calculation.
✓ symbol [source: symbol, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Adds HGNC gene symbols, which is critical for human-readable clinical interpretation.
✓ biotype [source: biotype, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Helps distinguish if the structural change affects a protein-coding gene, lincRNA, or other features.
✓ regulatory [source: regulatory, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Required to determine if the CNVs overlap with promoters, enhancers, or other regulatory elements.
✓ gnomad_sv [source: gnomad_sv, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Necessary to assess how common these structural variants are in healthy populations using gnomAD SV data.
✓ clinvar [source: clinvar, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Vital for clinical interpretation to see if similar structural variants have documented clinical significance.
✓ dosage_sensitivity [source: dosage_sensitivity, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Specifically helps interpret the impact of duplications/deletions (CNVs) by flagging dosage-sensitive genes.
✓ check_existing [source: check_existing, priority=optional] confidence: high
  Reason: Needed to resolve known variants and enable ClinVar/gnomAD lookup.
✓ phenotypes [source: phenotypes, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides clinical context by linking affected genes to known phenotypes.
✓ mane [source: mane, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Ensures clinical relevance by flagging MANE Select/Plus transcripts for consistent reporting.
✗ hgvs [source: hgvs] confidence: high
  Reason: HGVS nomenclature is typically applied to short variants; structural variant nomenclature is handled differently.
✗ sift [source: sift] confidence: high
  Reason: SIFT is a missense predictor for single nucleotide variants and is not applicable to structural/CNV changes.
✗ polyphen [source: polyphen] confidence: high
  Reason: PolyPhen is a missense predictor for single nucleotide variants and is not applicable to structural/CNV changes.
✗ cadd [source: cadd] confidence: high
  Reason: CADD scores are optimized for small indels and SNVs, not large-scale structural/CNV changes.
✗ most_severe [source: most_severe] confidence: high
  Reason: Disabling this to ensure full detail of all overlapping features (genes and regulatory elements) is provided.
✗ summary [source: summary] confidence: high
  Reason: Disabling this to avoid losing detailed annotation required for clinical-grade SV interpretation.

### 3. Generated VEP Command
```bash

```
