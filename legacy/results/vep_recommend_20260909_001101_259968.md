  Reason: Critical for CNVs (duplications/deletions) to determine if the affected gene is sensitive to changes in copy number.

  Reason: Recommended to identify if the structural change overlaps with previously known variants/IDs.

  Reason: Essential for clinical interpretation to see if the genomic region/variant has known clinical significance.

  Reason: Helps in clinical interpretation by linking affected genes to known clinical phenotypes.

  Reason: Provides high-confidence transcript identifiers (MANE Select) which are standard for clinical reporting.

  Reason: Helps prioritize results by providing Transcript Support Level for the overlapping transcripts.
✗ hgvs [source: hgvs] confidence: high
  Reason: HGVS nomenclature is primarily used for small variants (SNVs/indels); structural variant nomenclature is handled differently.
✗ sift [source: sift] confidence: high
  Reason: Missense predictors like SIFT are not applicable to structural variants (CNVs).
✗ polyphen [source: polyphen] confidence: high
  Reason: Missense predictors like PolyPhen are not applicable to structural variants (CNVs).
✗ cadd [source: cadd] confidence: high
  Reason: CADD scores are designed for small-scale variants (SNVs/indels) and are not appropriate for CNVs.
✗ most_severe [source: most_severe] confidence: high
  Reason: Disabling this ensures you see all overlapping gene/regulatory consequences, which is vital for structural variants that span multiple features.
✗ summary [source: summary] confidence: high
  Reason: A summary output would hide the detailed regulatory and gene-level overlaps required for this analysis.

### 3. Generated VEP Command
```bash
vep --input_file <input.vcf> --output_file <output.txt> --cache --format vcf --vcf --symbol --biotype --regulatory --gnomad_sv <path_to_gnomad_sv.vcf.gz> --dosage_sensitivity --check_existing --clinvar --phenotypes --mane --tsl --core_type ensembl
```
*Note: On the Ensembl Web Interface, ensure "Regulatory" is checked, "gnomAD SV" is selected from the custom datasets/plugins, and "Dosage Sensitivity" is enabled. Do not select "Most Severe" or "Summary" to ensure full overlap detail.*
