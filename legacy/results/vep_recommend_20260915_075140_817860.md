## Recommendation
### 1. Detected Scenario
- **species**: human (implied by "healthy people" context and clinical interpretation focus)
- **origin**: somatic ("tumour genome sequencing")
- **variant_size_class**: structural-CNV ("structural dna changes and gene duplications or deletions")
- **region_focus**: coding, regulatory-noncoding ("affect genes or regulatory regions")
- **analysis_goal**: population-frequency, clinical-interpretation ("check how common they are in healthy people for clinical interpretation")

### 2. Recommended Options
✓ core_type [source: core_type, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Necessary to define the underlying transcript/gene annotation set driving consequence calculation.
✓ symbol [source: symbol, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Adds HGNC gene symbols to the output, which is essential for human clinical interpretation.
✓ biotype [source: biotype, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Helps identify if the CNV affects protein-coding genes, lincRNA, or other functional elements.
✓ regulatory [source: regulatory, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Required to detect overlaps with enhancers, promoters, and CTCF binding sites as requested.
✓ gnomad_sv [source: gnomad_sv, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Specifically provides allele frequencies for structural variants from gnomAD to assess how common they are in healthy populations.
✓ dosage_sensitivity [source: dosage_sensitivity, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Critical for CNVs (duplications/deletions) as it annotates gene dosage sensitivity (e.g., pHaplo).
✓ check_existing [source: check_existing, priority=optional] confidence: high
  Reason: Allows the detection of known co-located variants.
✓ clinvar [source: clinvar, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Essential for clinical interpretation to see if the genomic region/variant has known clinical significance.
✓ phenotypes [source: phenotypes, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Links the affected genes to known phenotypes, aiding clinical context.
✓ canonical [source: canonical, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Helps prioritize the most representative transcript for a gene hit.
✓ mane [source: mane, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides MANE Select/Plus Clinical flags for standardized clinical reporting.
✓ tsl [source: tsl, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Adds Transcript Support Level to assess the reliability of the annotated transcripts.
✗ hgvs [source: hgvs] confidence: high
  Reason: HGVS nomenclature is primarily designed for small variants (SNVs/Indels) and is not typically applicable/useful for structural-CNV descriptions.
✗ dbnsfp [source: dbnsfp] confidence: high
  Reason: This plugin is for missense variants; it is NOT APPLICABLE for structural-CNV.
✗ sift [source: sift] confidence: high
  Reason: SIFT predicts impact of amino acid substitutions; NOT APPLICABLE for structural-CNV.
✗ polyphen [source: polyphen] confidence: high
  Reason: PolyPhen predicts impact of amino acid substitutions; NOT APPLICABLE for structural-CNV.
✗ cadd [source: cadd] confidence: high
  Reason: CADD is optimized for SNVs/Indels; NOT APPLICABLE for structural-CNV.
✗ most_severe [source: most_severe] confidence: high
  Reason: Disabling to allow full per-transcript/per-feature detail, which is necessary for understanding complex CNV overlaps.
✗ summary [source: summary] confidence: high
  Reason: Disabling to ensure all detailed annotations (regulatory, dosage, etc.) are preserved.
✗ coding_only [source: coding_only] confidence: high
  Reason: The user explicitly requested to see effects on regulatory regions, so coding-only restriction must be avoided.

### 3. Generated VEP Command
