I am reviewing human germline whole genome sequencing for a patient with suspected large-scale chromosomal changes. I need to quickly identify the impact on genes and regulatory regions, as well as how common these variants are in the general population.

### 2. Recommended Options
✓ core_type [source: core_type, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Standard requirement to drive consequence calculation using Ensembl/GENCODE annotations.
✓ symbol [source: symbol, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Essential for human-readable gene identification (e.g., BRCA1) during clinical review.
✓ biotype [source: biotype, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Necessary to distinguish between protein-coding genes and non-coding features (e.g., lincRNA) affected by the CNV.
✓ regulatory [source: regulatory, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Explicitly requested to assess impact on regulatory regions (promoters, enhancers, etc.).
✓ gnomad_sv [source: gnomad_sv, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Specifically required to assess the population frequency of structural variants using gnomAD SV data.
✓ dosage_sensitivity [source: dosage_sensitivity, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Highly relevant for CNV/structural variant analysis to determine if the gene is sensitive to copy number changes (pHaplo).
✓ check_existing [source: check_existing, priority=optional] confidence: medium
  Reason: Recommended to find co-located known variants/IDs to provide clinical context.
✓ phenotypes [source: phenotypes, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Helps link affected genes to known clinical phenotypes, aiding interpretation of the large-scale change.
✓ clinvar [source: clinvar, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Essential for clinical interpretation to see if the affected region or gene is associated with known pathogenicities.
✗ hgvs [source: hgvs] confidence: high
  Reason: HGVS nomenclature is primarily for short variants (SNVs/indels) and is not applicable to large structural/CNV changes.
✗ sift [source: sift] confidence: high
  Reason: SIFT is a missense predictor for single nucleotide changes and is not applicable to structural variants.
✗ polyphen [source: polyphen] confidence: high
  Reason: PolyPhen is a missense predictor for single nucleotide changes and is not applicable to structural variants.
✗ cadd [source: cadd] confidence: high
  Reason: CADD scores are designed for small variants and are not appropriate for large-scale chromosomal changes.
✗ most_severe [source: most_severe] confidence: high
  Reason: For structural variants, you need to see all overlapping features (genes, regulatory elements) rather than a single collapsed consequence.
✗ summary [source: summary] confidence: high
  Reason: Clinical review of CNVs requires full annotation of all impacted features, not a summary.

### 3. Generated VEP Command
```
vep --input_file <input.vcf> --output_file <output.txt> --cache --symbol --biotype --regulatory --gnomad_sv <path_to_gnomad_sv.vcf.gz> --plugin DosageSensitivity --plugin Phenotypes --check_existing --clinvar
```
*Note: The `--gnomad_sv` flag requires a local tabix-indexed VCF. In the Ensembl Web Interface, these options can be selected via the 'Plugins' and 'Additional Parameters' sections.*
