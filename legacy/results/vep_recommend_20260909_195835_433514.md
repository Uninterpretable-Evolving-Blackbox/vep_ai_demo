Human tumour genome sequencing with structural dna changes and gene duplications or deletions. I need to see if these affect genes or regulatory regions and check how common they are in healthy people for clinical interpretation.

- **region_focus**: coding, regulatory-noncoding (explicitly requested: "affect genes or regulatory regions")
- **analysis_goal**: population-frequency, clinical-interpretation (explicitly requested: "check how common they are in healthy people for clinical interpretation")

### 2. Recommended Options
✓ core_type [source: core_type, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Essential to define the transcript/gene annotation set driving the consequence calculation.
✓ symbol [source: symbol, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Makes results more human-interpretable by adding HGNC gene symbols.
✓ biotype [source: biotype, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Necessary to understand if the CNV affects protein-coding genes, lincRNA, etc.
✓ regulatory [source: regulatory, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Required to assess impacts on regulatory regions (promoters, enhancers) as requested.
✓ gnomad_sv [source: gnomad_sv, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Specifically provides allele frequencies for structural variants from gnomAD to assess how common they are in the population.
✓ dosage_sensitivity [source: dosage_sensitivity, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Critical for CNVs (duplications/deletions) to determine if the affected genes are sensitive to dosage changes (e.g., pHaplo scores).
✓ check_existing [source: check_existing, priority=optional] confidence: medium
  Reason: Useful to identify if the structural change overlaps with known variants in databases.
✓ clinvar [source: clinvar, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Vital for clinical interpretation to see if the genomic region/variant has known clinical significance.
✓ phenotypes [source: phenotypes, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Helps in clinical interpretation by linking affected genes to known phenotypes.
✗ hgvs [source: hgvs] confidence: high
  Reason: HGVS nomenclature is typically used for small variants (SNVs/Indels) rather than large structural/CNV changes.
✗ dbnsfp [source: dbnsfp] confidence: high
  Reason: This plugin is for missense variants; not applicable to structural/CNV changes.
✗ sift [source: sift] confidence: high
  Reason: Predicts impact of amino acid substitutions; not applicable to CNVs.
✗ polyphen [source: polyphen] confidence: high
  Reason: Predicts impact of amino acid substitutions; not applicable to CNVs.
✗ cadd [source: cadd] confidence: high
  Reason: CADD scores are designed for small variants (SNVs/Indels), not structural variants.
✗ most_severe [source: most_severe] confidence: high
  Reason: For CNVs, you need the full list of all overlapping genes and regulatory features, not just a single "most severe" term.
✗ summary [source: summary] confidence: high
  Reason: Summary mode collapses details; clinical interpretation of CNVs requires full per-transcript/feature detail.

### 3. Generated VEP Command
```bash
vep --input_file <input.vcf> --output_file <output.txt> --cache \
--dir_cache <path_to_cache> \
--plugin DosageSensitivity \
--plugin Phenotypes \
--custom <path_to_gnomAD_SV.vcf.gz>,gnomad_sv,exact \
--check_existing \
--symbol --biotype --regulatory --clinvar
```
*(Note: Web interface users should select 'Dosage Sensitivity', 'Phenotypes', 'gnomAD SV' in the custom/plugin sections and ensure 'Regulatory' and 'Gene Symbol' are checked.)*
