## User Query
I am working on a project involving human germline small variants, specifically SNVs and indels, identified from our whole-genome sequencing cohort. My primary goal is to perform clinical interpretation to identify potentially pathogenic variants, so I need to assess the impact on protein-coding sequences as well as any functional changes in regulatory or non-coding regions. How should I approach the setup to ensure I am capturing all these biological impacts and the necessary frequency data?
