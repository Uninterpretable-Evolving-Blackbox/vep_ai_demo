## User Query
I am starting to set up the pipeline for our human tumor-only whole genome sequencing data from a large cancer cohort, but I am still a bit unsure about how to best capture all the relevant information. We have identified several structural variants and copy number alterations, and my goal is to get a quick top-line summary of their functional consequences. Specifically, I need to see how these changes affect protein-coding regions—ideally identifying impacts on the most reliable transcripts—but I also want to capture any potential disruptions to regulatory or non-coding elements. Additionally, it would be really helpful if the results provided context regarding population allele frequencies so I can distinguish these somatic hits from common germline variation.
