  Reason: Provides gnomAD LOEUF scores, which are critical for assessing the constraint of genes affected by CNVs/SVs.

  Reason: Directly supports clinical interpretation by linking variants to cited medical literature.

  Reason: Adds HGNC gene symbols, making the output human-readable and useful for clinical reporting.

  Reason: Required to enable the retrieval of ClinVar and other existing variant annotations.

  Reason: Helps identify principal isoforms, which is crucial when interpreting the impact of a structural variant on a specific transcript.
✓ dosage_sensitivity [source: dosage_sensitivity, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Highly critical for CNVs/SVs, as it provides pHaplo/pLI scores to assess the impact of gene dosage changes.
✓ clinvar [source: clinvar, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides clinical significance (pathogenic/benign) for co-located variants, a primary requirement for clinical assessment.

  Reason: Identifies MANE Select/Plus transcripts, ensuring the clinical interpretation aligns with standardized NCBI/Ensembl transcripts.

  Reason: Links gene/variant hits to known phenotypes, supporting clinical diagnostic reasoning.

  Reason: Provides a combined deleteriousness score to help rank the impact of the variants.

### 3. Generated VEP Command
```
vep --input_file <input.vcf> --output_file <output.txt> --cache --format vcf --vcf --symbol --tsl --appris --mane --biotype --numbers --dosage_sensitivity --clinvar --mastermind --phenotypes --cadd --loeuf --frequency --check_existing --custom gnomad_sv,gnomad_sv.vcf.gz,exact
```
*Note: For the `--custom` flag, ensure you provide the actual path to your local gnomAD SV VCF file. On the Ensembl Web Interface, these correspond to enabling "Gene Symbol", "Transcript Support Level", "MANE", "ClinVar", "Dosage Sensitivity", and "gnomAD SV" in the advanced settings.*
