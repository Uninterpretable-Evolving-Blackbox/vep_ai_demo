# VEP Recommendation

**Date:** 2026-09-09T00:20:14.793700

## User Query
I am working with whole exome sequencing data from a cohort of zebrafish tumor models where I have identified several somatic SNVs and small indels by comparing the tumor DNA to matched germline samples. My primary goal is to identify which of these mutations in the protein-coding regions are likely to be pathogenic, so I need a way to assess their potential impact on the amino acid sequence and overall protein function to help prioritize candidate driver mutations for further study.

## Recommendation
### 1. Detected Scenario
- **species**: non-human (zebrafish)
- **origin**: somatic (tumor DNA vs matched germline)
- **variant_size_class**: small (SNVs and small indels)
- **region_focus**: coding (whole exome sequencing/protein-coding regions)
- **analysis_goal**: clinical-interpretation (prioritizing candidate driver mutations)
