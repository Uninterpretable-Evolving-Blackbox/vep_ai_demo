## User Query
I am currently reviewing exome sequencing results from a cohort of mice used in our recent developmental study. We have identified several single-letter changes and small additions or deletions throughout the genome. For my preliminary report, I just need to quickly identify which of these variations actually alter the protein sequence or create an early stop signal within the coding regions.

## Recommendation
### 1. Detected Scenario
- **species**: non-human (indicated by "mice")
- **origin**: somatic (implied by "identified... throughout the genome" in a study context, though could be germline; however, the goal is consequence identification)
- **variant_size_class**: small (indicated by "single-letter changes and small additions or deletions")
- **region_focus**: coding (indicated by "alter the protein sequence or create an early stop signal within the coding regions")
- **analysis_goal**: basic-consequence (indicated by "quickly identify which of these variations actually alter the protein sequence")

### 2. Recommended Options
✓ core_type [source: core_type, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides the primary Ensembl/GENCODE transcript/gene annotation driving the consequence calculation.
✓ symbol [source: symbol, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Adds gene symbols to the output, which is essential for identifying which genes are affected.
✓ hgvs [source: hgvs, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Adds HGVS nomenclature to the output, which is standard for describing protein-altering changes.
✓ protein [source: protein, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Adds Ensembl protein identifiers (ENSP) to link results to protein-level changes.
✓ biotype [source: biotype, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Adds the transcript biotype, helping to confirm the coding nature of the transcripts.
✓ canonical [source: canonical, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Identifies the canonical transcript, helping to prioritize the most relevant protein-altering effect.
✓ numbers [source: numbers, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Adds exon and intron numbering, which is useful for localizing changes within the coding sequence.
✓ domains [source: domains, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Adds overlapping protein domains, which helps assess the functional impact of a protein-altering change.
✗ most_severe [source: most_severe] confidence: high
  Reason: Disabling to allow full per-transcript detail; for protein-altering changes, knowing the specific transcript impact is more useful than a collapsed result.
✗ summary [source: summary] confidence: high
  Reason: Disabling to provide detailed consequence data rather than a condensed summary.
✗ af_gnomade [source: af_gnomade] confidence: high
  Reason: NOT APPLICABLE; gnomAD is for human species only.
✗ clinvar [source: clinvar] confidence: high
  Reason: NOT APPLICABLE; ClinVar is for human species only.
✗ polyphen [source: polyphen] confidence: high
  Reason: NOT APPLICABLE; PolyPhen is a human-only tool.
✗ cadd [source: cadd] confidence: high
  Reason: NOT APPLICABLE; CADD is a human-only resource.
✗ dbnsfp [source: dbnsfp] confidence: high
  Reason: NOT APPLICABLE; dbNSFP is for human species only.

