### 2. Recommended Options
✓ core_type [source: core_type, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Standard Ensembl/GENCODE core annotation is required to drive all consequence calculations.
✓ symbol [source: symbol, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Adds HGNC gene symbols to make results human-readable and easier to interpret.
✓ biotype [source: biotype, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Essential to distinguish if the SV hits a protein-coding gene, lincRNA, or other regulatory feature.
✓ regulatory [source: regulatory, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Needed to identify overlaps with promoters, enhancers, and other regulatory elements as requested.
✓ gnomad_sv [source: gnomad_sv, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Specifically designed to annotate structural variants with gnomAD SV allele frequencies to filter common variants.
✓ canonical [source: canonical, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Helps identify the primary transcript of interest for any genes disrupted by the CNV.
✓ dosage_sensitivity [source: dosage_sensitivity, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Highly relevant for CNVs/structural variants to determine if the affected gene is sensitive to copy-number changes (pHaplo).
✓ tsl [source: tsl, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides Transcript Support Level to assess the quality/evidence of the transcripts being overlapped.
✓ numbers [source: numbers, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides exon/intron numbering which is helpful for localizing the breakpoints of the CNV.
✗ hgvs [source: hgvs] confidence: high
  Reason: HGVS nomenclature is primarily designed for small variants (SNVs/indels) and is not appropriate for CNVs/structural variants.
✗ dbnsfp [source: dbnsfp] confidence: high
  Reason: This resource aggregates missense predictors (SIFT, PolyPhen) which do not apply to structural variant consequences.
✗ sift [source: sift] confidence: high
  Reason: Missense predictors are not applicable to structural variants or CNVs.
✗ polyphen [source: polyphen] confidence: high
  Reason: Missense predictors are not applicable to structural variants or CNVs.
✗ cadd [source: cadd] confidence: high
  Reason: CADD is a missense/small-indel pathogenicity predictor and is not applicable to CNVs.
✗ most_severe [source: most_severe] confidence: medium
  Reason: For structural variants, it is often important to see all overlapping features (coding and regulatory) rather than collapsing to one term.
✗ summary [source: summary] confidence: medium
  Reason: User wants to identify consequences across both coding and regulatory regions; a summary would hide these detailed overlaps.
✗ pick [source: pick] confidence: medium
  Reason: Using --pick would restrict results to one consequence, potentially hiding the important regulatory consequences in favor of a coding one (or vice versa).

