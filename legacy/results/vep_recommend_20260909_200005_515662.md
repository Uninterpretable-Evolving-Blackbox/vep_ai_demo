### 2. Recommended Options
✓ core_type [source: core_type, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Essential to drive consequence calculation using Ensembl/GENCODE annotations.
✓ symbol [source: symbol, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Adds HGNC gene symbols, making results more human-readable for clinical interpretation.
✓ biotype [source: biotype, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Helps identify if the SV overlaps protein-coding genes or other transcript types.
✓ dosage_sensitivity [source: dosage_sensitivity, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Critical for CNVs; annotates genes with dosage sensitivity scores (e.g., pHaplo) from the Collins rCNV catalogue.
✓ gnomad_sv [source: gnomad_sv, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides gnomAD structural-variant allele frequencies to help filter common benign CNVs.
✓ check_existing [source: check_existing, priority=optional] confidence: high
  Reason: Required to enable ClinVar and frequency lookups for co-located variants.
✓ clinvar [source: clinvar, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides clinical significance for co-located variants, essential for pathogenicity assessment.
✓ phenotypes [source: phenotypes, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Retrieves phenotype associations for the affected genes to aid clinical correlation.
✗ hgvs [source: hgvs] confidence: high
  Reason: HGVS nomenclature is primarily designed for short variants (SNVs/Indels) and is not applicable to structural variants/CNVs.
✗ dbnsfp [source: dbnsfp] confidence: high
  Reason: dbNSFP is a meta-resource for missense variants; not applicable to structural variants.
✗ sift [source: sift] confidence: high
  Reason: SIFT predicts the effect of amino acid substitutions; not applicable to CNVs/SVs.
✗ polyphen [source: polyphen] confidence: high
  Reason: PolyPhen predicts the effect of amino acid substitutions; not applicable to CNVs/SVs.
✗ cadd [source: cadd] confidence: high
  Reason: CADD scores are designed for SNVs/Indels; not applicable to structural variants.
✗ most_severe [source: most_severe] confidence: high
  Reason: Clinical SV analysis requires seeing all overlapping features/genes, not just a single collapsed consequence.
✗ summary [source: summary] confidence: high
  Reason: Summary mode hides the detailed transcript-level information needed for clinical review.

### 3. Generated VEP Command
