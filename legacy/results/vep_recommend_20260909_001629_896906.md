  Reason: Adds HGVS nomenclature for standardized reporting of the variants.

  Reason: Necessary to identify overlaps with regulatory regions like promoters and enhancers, which is the primary goal.

  Reason: Surfaces phenotype/disease associations for genes, which helps in interpreting the clinical relevance of a mutation.

  Reason: CADD is a human-only resource.
