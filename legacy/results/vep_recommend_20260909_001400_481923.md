  Reason: Specifically required to annotate structural variants with gnomAD structural-variant allele frequencies to assess population prevalence.

  Reason: Crucial for CNVs, as this plugin annotates genes with dosage sensitivity scores (e.g., pHaplo) from the ClinCNV catalogue.

  Reason: Provides exon and intron numbering which helps in localizing the boundaries of the deletion/duplication.

  Reason: HGVS nomenclature is primarily for short variants; not applicable/standard for structural CNVs in this context.

✗ dbnsfp [source: dbnsfp] confidence: high
  Reason: This resource aggregates data for missense variants, which is not applicable to structural CNVs.

✗ sift [source: sift] confidence: high
  Reason: SIFT predicts the impact of amino acid substitutions, not structural variations.

✗ polyphen [source: polyphen] confidence: high
  Reason: PolyPhen predicts the impact of missense changes, not structural variations.

✗ cadd [source: cadd] confidence: high
  Reason: CADD is designed for single nucleotide and small indel pathogenicity, not large-scale CNVs.

✗ most_severe [source: most_severe] confidence: high
  Reason: For structural variants, you need the full per-transcript detail to see which specific coding regions are lost or gained, rather than a collapsed summary.

✗ summary [source: summary] confidence: high
  Reason: A summary output hides the specific transcript/exon details necessary for CNV interpretation.
