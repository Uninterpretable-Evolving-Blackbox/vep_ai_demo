# VEP Recommendation

**Date:** 2026-09-10T00:38:33.872791

## User Query
I am working with whole exome sequencing data from a cohort of zebrafish tumor models where I have identified several somatic SNVs and small indels by comparing the tumor DNA to matched germline samples. My primary goal is to identify which of these mutations in the protein-coding regions are likely to be pathogenic, so I need a way to assess their potential impact on the amino acid sequence and overall protein function to help prioritize candidate driver mutations for further study.

## Recommendation
### 1. Detected Scenario
- **species**: non-human (zebrafish)
- **origin**: somatic (tumor DNA compared to matched germline)
- **variant_size_class**: small (SNVs and small indels)
- **region_focus**: coding (protein-coding regions/WES)
- **analysis_goal**: clinical-interpretation (identifying pathogenic/driver mutations)

### 2. Recommended Options
✓ biotype [source: biotype, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Adds the transcript biotype to help identify protein-coding vs non-coding features.
✓ canonical [source: canonical, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Helps prioritize the most reliable transcript for determining the impact of the mutation.
✓ check_existing [source: check_existing, priority=optional] confidence: medium
  Reason: Useful to see if these somatic variants have been previously catalogued in known databases.
✓ core_type [source: core_type, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Essential for selecting the primary annotation set that drives consequence calculation.
✓ domains [source: domains, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Adds protein domain information (Pfam, etc.), which is critical for assessing impact on protein function in a clinical/driver context.
✓ hgvs [source: hgvs, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides standard nomenclature for the amino acid changes, essential for reporting and downstream validation.
✓ maxentscan [source: maxentscan, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides sequence-based scoring for splice site impact, which is important for small indels/SNVs.
✓ numbers [source: numbers, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides exon/intron numbering, which is vital for characterizing the location of the mutation within the gene.
✓ phenotypes [source: phenotypes, priority=recommended] confidence: medium
  Reason: Retrieves known phenotype associations to assist in interpreting potential driver function.
✓ protein [source: protein, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Adds protein identifiers (ENSP) to facilitate linking variants to protein-level functional studies.
✓ symbol [source: symbol, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Adds gene symbols to make the output human-readable and easier to interpret biologically.
✓ sift [source: sift, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides species-specific SIFT scores to predict the impact of missense substitutions on protein function.
✗ most_severe [source: most_severe] confidence: high
  Reason: Disabled because it conflicts with several recommended options like hgvs, symbol, and sift.

  Reason: NOT APPLICABLE; ClinVar contains human clinical significance data.
