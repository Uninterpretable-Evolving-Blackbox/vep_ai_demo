### 2. Recommended Options
✓ core_type [source: core_type, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Essential to provide the baseline Ensembl/GENCODE annotation required for all downstream analysis.
✓ symbol [source: symbol, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Necessary to provide human-readable gene symbols (e.g., BRCA1) for clinical interpretation.
✓ biotype [source: biotype, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Helps distinguish if the CNV affects protein-coding genes, lincRNA, or other functional elements.
✓ regulatory [source: regulatory, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Specifically requested to identify impacts on regulatory regions (promoters, enhancers, etc.).
✓ gnomad_sv [source: gnomad_sv, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Crucial for determining population frequency of structural variants using gnomAD-SV data.
✓ dosage_sensitivity [source: dosage_sensitivity, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Highly relevant for CNVs/structural variants to identify genes where copy-number changes cause disease (e.g., pHaplo).
✓ check_existing [source: check_existing, priority=optional] confidence: medium
  Reason: Recommended to identify if these large-scale changes are already catalogued in known databases.
✓ clinvar [source: clinvar, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Required for clinical interpretation to see if the identified structural changes have known pathogenic significance.
✓ phenotypes [source: phenotypes, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Helps link the impacted genes to specific clinical phenotypes/diseases.
✗ most_severe [source: most_severe] confidence: high
  Reason: Should be disabled to ensure full per-transcript/per-feature detail is preserved for clinical review.
✗ summary [source: summary] confidence: high
  Reason: Should be disabled to avoid losing detailed annotation necessary for structural variant interpretation.
✗ sift [source: sift] confidence: high
  Reason: Missense predictors like SIFT are not applicable to structural variants/CNVs.
✗ polyphen [source: polyphen] confidence: high
  Reason: Missense predictors like PolyPhen are not applicable to structural variants/CNVs.
✗ cadd [source: cadd] confidence: high
  Reason: CADD is primarily designed for small variants (SNVs/indels) and is not applicable to large-scale CNVs.

### 3. Generated VEP Command
```bash
vep --input_file <input.vcf> --output_file <output.txt> --cache --format vcf --vcf --everything --symbol --biotype --regulatory --plugin DosageSensitivity --plugin gnomad_sv --custom <path_to_gnomad_sv.vcf>,type=exact,overlap_cutoff=80 --check_existing --plugin ClinVar --plugin Phenotypes
```
*Note: In the Ensembl Web Interface, enable "Gene symbol", "Transcript biotype", "Regulatory regions", "Dosage sensitivity", "gnomAD SV", "ClinVar", and "Phenotypes". Do not select "Most severe" or "Summary".*
