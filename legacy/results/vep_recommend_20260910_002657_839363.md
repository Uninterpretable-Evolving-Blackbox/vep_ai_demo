## User Query
I am working on a project involving human germline small variants, specifically SNVs and indels, identified from our whole-genome sequencing cohort. My primary goal is to perform clinical interpretation to identify potentially pathogenic variants, so I need to assess the impact on protein-coding sequences as well as any functional changes in regulatory or non-coding regions. Additionally, it is crucial for my analysis that I can incorporate population allele frequency context to help distinguish rare, potentially deleterious variants from common polymorphisms. How should I approach the setup to ensure I am capturing all these biological impacts and the necessary frequency data?

### 3. Generated VEP Command
```bash
vep --input_file <input.vcf> --output_file <output.txt> --cache --format vcf --vcf --everything \
--plugin ClinPred --plugin CADD --plugin SpliceAI --plugin AlphaMissense --plugin REVEL \
--plugin UTRAnnotator --plugin MaveDB --plugin EVE --plugin Phenotypes \
--af_gnomade --af_gnomadg --af_1kg --check_existing --regulatory --symbol --hgvs --protein --mane --appris --tsl --domains --numbers
```
*(Note: `--everything` is a shorthand for many common flags, but for a precise clinical pipeline, explicit flags as listed above are preferred. The `--plugin` paths should be configured in your environment or passed via `--dir_plugins`.)*
