### 2. Recommended Options
✓ core_type [source: core_type, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides the foundational Ensembl/GENCODE gene annotation required for all consequence calculations.
✓ symbol [source: symbol, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Adds HGNC gene symbols, which are essential for human clinical interpretation and linking to oncology databases.
✓ biotype [source: biotype, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Helps distinguish if the CNV/SV impacts protein-coding genes vs other features.
✓ dosage_sensitivity [source: dosage_sensitivity, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Crucial for CNVs; annotates genes with dosage sensitivity scores (e.g., pHaplo) from the Collins rCNV 2022 catalogue.
✓ gnomad_sv [source: gnomad_sv, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides allele frequencies for structural variants from gnomAD, essential for filtering common benign SVs.
✓ clinvar [source: clinvar, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Required for the analysis goal of determining clinical pathogenicity.
✓ check_existing [source: check_existing, priority=optional] confidence: high
  Reason: Necessary to link the input variants to ClinVar and gnomAD-SV data.
✓ phenotypes [source: phenotypes, priority=recommended] confidence: medium
  Reason: Helps with clinical interpretation by retrieving phenotype associations for the hit genes.
✗ hgvs [source: hgvs] confidence: high
  Reason: HGVS nomenclature is primarily designed for small variants (SNVs/Indels) and is not applicable to structural variants/CNVs.
✗ dbnsfp [source: dbnsfp] confidence: high
  Reason: dbNSFP is a resource for missense/small variants; not applicable to structural variants.
✗ sift [source: sift] confidence: high
  Reason: SIFT predicts effects of amino acid substitutions; not applicable to CNVs/SVs.
✗ polyphen [source: polyphen] confidence: high
  Reason: PolyPhen predicts effects of amino acid substitutions; not applicable to CNVs/SVs.
✗ cadd [source: cadd] confidence: high
  Reason: CADD is optimized for small variants; not applicable to structural variants.
✗ spliceai [source: spliceai] confidence: high
  Reason: SpliceAI predicts splice-site alterations for small variants; not applicable to CNVs/SVs.
✗ most_severe [source: most_severe] confidence: high
  Reason: For structural variants, you need to see all overlapping genes and consequences rather than a single collapsed line.
✗ summary [source: summary] confidence: high
  Reason: Clinical interpretation of CNVs requires detailed per-transcript/per-gene information, not a summary.

### 3. Generated VEP Command
```bash
vep --input_file <input.vcf> --output_file <output.txt> --cache --plugin DosageSensitivity --plugin gnomad_sv,/path/to/gnomad_sv.vcf.gz --check_existing --clinvar --phenotypes --symbol --biotype
```
*Note: On the Ensembl VEP web interface, ensure you select "Human" and check the boxes for Gene Symbol, Biotype, ClinVar, and use the Custom plugin section for DosageSensitivity and gnomAD SV.*
