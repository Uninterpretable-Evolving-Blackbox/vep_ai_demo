### 2. Recommended Options
✓ core_type [source: core_type, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Uses Ensembl/GENCODE core annotations which are the standard for driving consequence calculation in human studies.

✓ symbol [source: symbol, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Adds HGNC gene symbols to make results human-readable and clinically relevant.

✓ tsl [source: tsl, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides Transcript Support Level to help assess the reliability of the transcript annotations.

✓ appris [source: appris, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Identifies functionally important isoforms, crucial for interpreting the impact of structural changes on protein function.

✓ canonical [source: canonical, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Flags the canonical transcript, helping to prioritize the most biologically relevant consequence.

✓ biotype [source: biotype, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Essential to confirm that the CNV/SV affects protein-coding genes versus non-coding elements.

✓ dosage_sensitivity [source: dosage_sensitivity, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Highly relevant for CNVs; annotates gene dosage sensitivity (e.g., haploinsufficiency) from the Collins rCNV 2022 catalogue.

✓ mane [source: mane, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Flags MANE Select/Plus transcripts, providing a standardized reference for clinical interpretation.

✓ numbers [source: numbers, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides exon/intron numbering, aiding in the localization of the variant within the gene structure.

✓ phenotype [source: phenotypes, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Connects genes/variants to known phenotypes, aiding clinical interpretation.

✓ loeuf [source: loeuf, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides gnomAD LOEUF scores to help distinguish between constrained and unconstrained genes.

✓ mastermind [source: mastermind, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Links variants to clinical evidence in medical literature, directly supporting the clinical interpretation goal.

✓ clinvar [source: clinvar, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides ClinVar clinical significance to help identify known pathogenic structural/coding variants.

✓ cadd [source: cadd, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides a combined deleteriousness score to help rank the impact of the variants.

✓ gnomad_sv [source: gnomad_sv, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Specifically addresses the structural variant/CNV aspect by providing gnomAD SV allele frequencies.

✓ check_existing [source: check_existing, priority=optional] confidence: medium
  Reason: Enabled to allow ClinVar and other population data to be pulled via co-location.

✓ go [source: go, priority=optional] confidence: medium
  Reason: Adds biological process context via Gene Ontology, which aids in functional interpretation.

✗ hgvs [source: hgvs] confidence: high
  Reason: Not recommended because it conflicts with the priority to provide detailed symbol/transcript annotations in a summary-style workflow.

✗ most_severe [source: most_severe] confidence: high
  Reason: Disabled to allow for full annotation of all consequences, which is necessary for SV/CNV analysis where multiple overlapping effects may occur.

✗ coding_only [source: coding_only] confidence: high
  Reason: Although the focus is coding, SVs often have effects in flanking or regulatory regions; restricting to coding only might miss critical context.

✗ dbnsfp [source: dbnsfp] confidence: high
  Reason: Primarily used for missense SNPs; less relevant for the primary goal of SV/CNV interpretation compared to Dosage Sensitivity.

✗ spliceai [source: spliceai] confidence: high
  Reason: Primarily for SNV-driven splicing changes; less applicable to large-scale structural variants.

### 3. Generated VEP Command
```
vep --input_file <input.vcf> --output_file <output.txt> --cache --symbol --tsl --appris --canonical --biotype --dosage_sensitivity --mane --numbers --plugin Phenotypes --plugin LOEUF --plugin Mastermind --check_existing --clinvar --plugin CADD --custom gnomAD_SV,file,<path_to_gnomad_sv.vcf>,type=exact,overlap_cutoff=exact --plugin GO
```
*(Note: `--check_existing` is required to enable `--clinvar`. The `--custom` flag for `gnomad_sv` requires a local VCF file path.)*
