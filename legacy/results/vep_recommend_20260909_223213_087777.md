I have human germline WGS structural variants and CNVs; I want to assess their impact on protein-coding transcripts and population allele frequency for clinical pathogenicity assessment.

- **species**: human ("human germline WGS")

- **variant_size_class**: structural-CNV ("structural variants and CNVs")
- **region_focus**: coding ("impact on protein-coding transcripts")
- **analysis_goal**: clinical-interpretation, population-frequency ("clinical pathogenicity assessment" and "population allele frequency")

### 2. Recommended Options
✓ appris [source: appris, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Essential for clinical interpretation to identify the principal, functionally most important isoforms.
✗ alphamissense [source: alphamissense] confidence: high
  Reason: This is a missense-specific tool; not applicable to structural variants/CNVs.
✓ biotype [source: biotype, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides necessary context regarding the type of gene or transcript being affected.
✓ check_existing [source: check_existing, priority=optional] confidence: high
  Reason: Required to link the input to known variants and ClinVar/population data.
✓ clinvar [source: clinvar, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Directly addresses the goal of clinical pathogenicity assessment by providing known clinical significance.
✓ core_type [source: core_type, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Standard practice to use the Ensembl/GENCODE core annotation for driving consequences.
✓ cadd [source: cadd, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides integrated deleteriousness scores which assist in pathogenicity assessment.
✓ dosage_sensitivity [source: dosage_sensitivity, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Highly relevant for CNVs/structural variants to determine if the gene is sensitive to dosage changes (e.g., pHaplo).
✓ frequency [source: frequency, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Directly addresses the requirement to assess population allele frequency.
✓ gnomad_sv [source: gnomad_sv, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Specifically designed to annotate structural variants with gnomAD SV allele frequencies.
✓ mane [source: mane, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides MANE Select/Plus Clinical flags to ensure clinical certainty in transcript selection.
✓ numbers [source: numbers, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides exon/intron numbering which is crucial for describing the impact of a CNV.
✓ symbol [source: symbol, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Adds HGNC gene symbols to make the output human-readable and clinically useful.
✓ tsl [source: tsl, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Adds Transcript Support Level to help assess the reliability of the transcript annotations.

*Note: Options like `sift`, `polyphen`, and `revel` are disabled as they are specific to single nucleotide missense variants and are not applicable to structural variants/CNVs.*

### 3. Generated VEP Command
```
vep --input_file <input.vcf> --output_file <output.txt> --cache --plugin ClinPred --plugin DosageSensitivity --plugin CADD --plugin gnomad_sv --symbol --mane --tsl --appris --biotype --numbers --frequency --check_existing --clinvar
```
*(Note: Ensure the `--plugin gnomad_sv` path points to the local VCF file for the gnomAD SV dataset. On the Ensembl Web Interface, these correspond to the checkboxes for Symbol, MANE, TSL, Appris, Biotype, Numbers, Frequency, ClinVar, Dosage Sensitivity, and CADD.)*
