  Reason: Provides standard HGVS nomenclature for coding variants, which is critical for downstream functional interpretation.

✓ symbol [source: symbol, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Adds gene symbols to make the output more readable and biologically interpretable.
✓ regulatory [source: regulatory, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Directly requested to assess the impact on regulatory regions.
✓ canonical [source: canonical, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Helps prioritize consequences by identifying if they hit the canonical transcript.
✓ protein [source: protein, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Adds Ensembl protein identifiers (ENSP), necessary for linking coding variants to protein-level consequences.
