## User Query
I am working on a new project involving whole genome sequencing data from a large cohort of wild-type *Mus musculus* and I am trying to figure out how to properly set up my annotation pipeline. We have identified many germline SNVs and small indels, but since my research focuses specifically on the impact of variants in non-coding and regulatory regions like enhancers, I am struggling with the configuration. My goal is just to get a quick top-line assessment of whether these small variants fall within functional non-coding elements or have predicted effects in those areas.

  Reason: Essential for providing the base Ensembl/GENCODE transcript and gene annotation driving the consequence calculation.

  Reason: Specifically required to identify overlaps with regulatory regions (promoters, enhancers, etc.) which is the user's primary focus.

  Reason: Helps distinguish between different types of non-coding features (e.g., lincRNA, miRNA) to provide context for the regulatory hits.

  Reason: Recommended to identify the primary transcript for a gene, providing a standard reference for functional context.

  Reason: Adds gene symbols to make the output interpretable for a top-line assessment.

  Reason: gnomAD is human-only and not applicable to *Mus musculus*.

  Reason: dbNSFP is a human-only meta-resource.

### 3. Generated VEP Command
```bash
vep --input_file input.vcf --output_file output.txt --cache --format vcf --vcf --species mus_musculus --core_type ensembl --regulatory --biotype --canonical --symbol
```
*Note: The `--regulatory` option requires the Ensembl Regulatory Build cache for the specific species to be downloaded/available. On the Ensembl Web Interface, these correspond to the "Regulatory" and "Biotype" checkboxes.*
