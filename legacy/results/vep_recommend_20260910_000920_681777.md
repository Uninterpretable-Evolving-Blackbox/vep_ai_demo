**Date:** 2026-09-10T00:09:20.682112

## User Query
I am starting to set up the pipeline for our human tumor-only whole genome sequencing data from a large cancer cohort, but I am still a bit unsure about how to best capture all the relevant information. We have identified several structural variants and copy number alterations, and my goal is to get a quick top-line summary of their functional consequences. Specifically, I need to see how these changes affect protein-coding regions—ideally identifying impacts on the most reliable transcripts—but I also want to capture any potential disruptions to regulatory or non-coding elements. Additionally, it would be really helpful if the results provided context regarding population allele frequencies so I can distinguish these somatic hits from common germline variation.

## Recommendation
### 1. Detected Scenario
- **species**: human ("human tumor-only")
- **origin**: somatic ("tumor-only... somatic hits")
- **variant_size_class**: structural-CNV ("structural variants and copy number alterations")
- **region_focus**: coding, regulatory-noncoding ("affect protein-coding regions... disruptions to regulatory or non-coding elements")
- **analysis_goal**: basic-consequence, population-frequency ("top-line summary of their functional consequences... context regarding population allele frequencies")

### 2. Recommended Options
✓ biotype [source: biotype, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Essential for categorizing the type of feature hit (e.g., protein_coding vs lincRNA), especially when looking at non-coding/regulatory regions.
✓ check_existing [source: check_existing, priority=optional] confidence: high
  Reason: Required to enable the retrieval of population frequency data and known variant IDs.
✓ core_type [source: core_type, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Sets the primary annotation driving the consequence calculation for the human genome.
✓ dosage_sensitivity [source: dosage_sensitivity, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Highly relevant for structural variants and CNVs to determine if the affected gene is sensitive to dosage changes.
✓ gnomad_sv [source: gnomad_sv, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Specifically addresses the user's need to assess structural variants/CNVs against population frequency data.
✓ numbers [source: numbers, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides exon/intron numbering, which is helpful for a "top-line" understanding of where the disruption occurs.
✓ regulatory [source: regulatory, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Necessary to fulfill the requirement of capturing disruptions to regulatory or non-coding elements.
✓ symbol [source: symbol, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Adds HGNC gene symbols, making the "top-line" summary human-readable and clinically relevant.
✓ tsl [source: tsl, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Helps identify the "most reliable transcripts" by providing Transcript Support Level (TSL) rankings.
✓ appris [source: appris, priority=recommended] confidence: medium
  Reason: Assists in identifying the most functionally important isoforms, aiding the user's goal of focusing on reliable transcripts.
✗ af_gnomade [source: af_gnomade, priority=NOT APPLICABLE] confidence: high
  Reason: The user is looking for general population context; gnomAD exome-only frequencies are too narrow for WGS-based CNV analysis.
✗ af_gnomadg [source: af_gnomadg, priority=NOT APPLICABLE] confidence: high
  Reason: While gnomAD genome is useful, the specific gnomad_sv plugin is more appropriate for the structural variant focus of this scenario.
✗ hgvs [source: hgvs, priority=NOT APPLICABLE] confidence: high
  Reason: Conflicts with the goal of a "top-line summary" and is generally less useful for structural/CNV-focused analysis.
✗ most_severe [source: most_severe, priority=optional] confidence: medium
  Reason: While useful for a summary, it conflicts with many helpful annotation options like symbol and tsl; better to provide full context for CNVs.
✗ coding_only [source: coding_only, priority=NOT APPLICABLE] confidence: high
  Reason: The user explicitly requested to capture regulatory and non-coding elements.

### 3. Generated VEP Command
```
vep --input_file <input.vcf> --output_file <output.txt> --cache --plugin DosageSensitivity --plugin gnomad_sv --symbol --tsl --regulatory --biotype --numbers --appris
```
*(Note: In the web interface, ensure you enable the "DosageSensitivity" and "gnomad_sv" plugins and select the "Ensembl Regulatory Build" to see non-coding consequences.)*
