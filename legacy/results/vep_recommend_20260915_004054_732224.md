## User Query
I am processing human germline sequencing data from a large-scale population cohort and have identified several large structural changes, such as deletions and duplications of DNA segments. I want to focus my analysis on these changes when they occur in regulatory regions that control gene activity, rather than just looking at the protein-coding parts of the genome. For these types of variations, I need to ensure my annotation process includes information on how common these changes are within the general population so I can identify which ones are truly rare.

## Recommendation
### 1. Detected Scenario
- **species**: human ("human germline sequencing data")
- **origin**: germline ("germline sequencing data")
- **variant_size_class**: structural-CNV ("large structural changes, such as deletions and duplications")
- **region_focus**: regulatory-noncoding ("regulatory regions that control gene activity, rather than just looking at the protein-coding parts")
- **analysis_goal**: population-frequency ("include information on how common these changes are within the general population")

### 2. Recommended Options
✓ biotype [source: biotype, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Adds the biotype of the regulatory feature, which is essential for characterizing non-coding elements.
✓ canonical [source: canonical, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Helps identify the most reliable transcript/feature for the genomic location.
✓ check_existing [source: check_existing, priority=optional] confidence: high
  Reason: Necessary to enable the retrieval of population frequency data and clinical significance for co-located variants.
✓ dosage_sensitivity [source: dosage_sensitivity, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Critical for CNV analysis to determine if a gene is sensitive to changes in copy number.
✓ frequency [source: frequency, priority=optional] confidence: high
  Reason: Enables frequency-based filtering/reporting to help distinguish rare variants from common polymorphisms.
✓ gnomad_sv [source: gnomad_sv, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides allele frequencies specifically for structural variants from gnomAD, which is the core requirement for this analysis.
✓ regulatory [source: regulatory, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Essential to annotate overlaps with regulatory regions (promoters, enhancers) as requested.
✓ symbol [source: symbol, priority=NOT APPLICABLE] confidence: high
  Reason: While useful, it is not specifically prioritized for purely non-coding regulatory focus compared to biotype, though often used; however, per instructions, I will only enable recommended/optional.

  Reason: Focuses on exome populations, whereas structural variants are better captured by gnomAD genome (gnomad_sv).
