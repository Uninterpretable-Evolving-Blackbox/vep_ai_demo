### 2. Recommended Options
✓ core_type [source: core_type, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Essential to provide the base Ensembl/GENCODE transcript/gene annotation set.
✓ symbol [source: symbol, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Necessary to identify which genes are affected by the chromosomal changes.
✓ biotype [source: biotype, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Helps distinguish if the structural variant affects protein-coding genes, lincRNAs, or other features.
✓ regulatory [source: regulatory, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Explicitly requested to identify impact on regulatory regions (promoters, enhancers, etc.).
✓ gnomad_sv [source: gnomad_sv, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Critical for determining the population frequency of structural variants using the gnomAD SV dataset.
✓ dosage_sensitivity [source: dosage_sensitivity, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Highly relevant for CNVs to determine if the gene is sensitive to changes in copy number (e.g., pHaplo).
✓ check_existing [source: check_existing, priority=optional] confidence: medium
  Reason: Required to link the structural variants to known database entries and ClinVar.
✓ clinvar [source: clinvar, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Essential for clinical interpretation to see if the identified change has known clinical significance.
✓ phenotypes [source: phenotypes, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Helps link the affected genes to known clinical phenotypes/diseases.
✓ canonical [source: canonical, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides context on whether the variant hits the primary functional transcript.
✓ mane [source: mane, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Ensures consistency with NCBI/Ensembl standard transcripts for clinical reporting.
✗ hgvs [source: hgvs] confidence: high
  Reason: HGVS nomenclature is primarily for short variants (SNVs/indels) and is not applicable to large-scale structural changes.
✗ sift [source: sift] confidence: high
  Reason: Missense predictors like SIFT are for single amino acid substitutions, not large-scale CNVs.
✗ polyphen [source: polyphen] confidence: high
  Reason: Missense predictors like PolyPhen are for single amino acid substitutions, not large-scale CNVs.
✗ cadd [source: cadd] confidence: high
  Reason: CADD scores are designed for small-scale variants, not large-scale structural variations.
✗ most_severe [source: most_severe] confidence: high
  Reason: For structural variants, we need full transcript/feature overlap details, not a single collapsed consequence.
✗ summary [source: summary] confidence: high
  Reason: Clinical review of CNVs requires detailed annotation rather than a condensed summary.

### 3. Generated VEP Command
```bash
vep --input_file <input.vcf> --output_file <output.txt> --cache --core_type merged --symbol --biotype --regulatory --gnomad_sv <path_to_gnomad_sv.vcf.gz> --plugin DosageSensitivity --check_existing --clinvar --plugin Phenotypes --canonical --mane --transcript_version
```
*(Note: `--transcript_version` is recommended for clinical reproducibility. On the Ensembl Web Interface, ensure "Regulatory" and "gnomAD SV" are selected in the plugins/options sections.)*
