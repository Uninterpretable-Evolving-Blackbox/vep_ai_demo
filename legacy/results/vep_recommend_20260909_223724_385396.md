I am reviewing human germline whole genome sequencing for a patient with suspected large-scale chromosomal changes. I need to quickly identify the impact on genes and regulatory regions, as well as how common these variants are in the general population.

- **analysis_goal**: clinical-interpretation, population-frequency (indicated by "identify the impact" and "how common these variants are")

### 2. Recommended Options
✓ core_type [source: core_type, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Uses the default Ensembl/GENCODE transcript set to drive consequence calculation.
✓ symbol [source: symbol, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Essential for clinical interpretation to provide human-readable HGNC gene symbols.
✓ biotype [source: biotype, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Helps distinguish if the CNV affects protein-coding genes, lincRNA, or other features.
✓ regulatory [source: regulatory, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Required to identify impacts on promoters, enhancers, and other non-coding regulatory regions.
✓ gnomad_sv [source: gnomad_sv, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Specifically annotates structural variants with gnomAD SV allele frequencies to assess population rarity.
✓ dosage_sensitivity [source: dosage_sensitivity, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Crucial for CNVs to identify if the affected genes are sensitive to copy-number changes (e.g., haploinsufficiency).
✓ check_existing [source: check_existing, priority=optional] confidence: medium
  Reason: Necessary to cross-reference with known variants and ClinVar.
✓ clinvar [source: clinvar, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides clinical significance for co-located variants to aid in diagnostic interpretation.
✓ phenotypes [source: phenotypes, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Links affected genes to known clinical phenotypes/diseases.
✓ numbers [source: numbers, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides exon/intron numbering to help localize the disruption within a gene.
✓ tsl [source: tsl, priority=recommended] confidence: medium
  Reason: Adds Transcript Support Level to help prioritize high-confidence transcripts.
✓ appris [source: appris, priority=recommended] confidence: medium
  Reason: Helps identify functionally important isoforms via the APPRIS system.
✓ mane [source: mane, priority=recommended] confidence: medium
  Reason: Identifies MANE Select transcripts for standardized clinical reporting.
✗ hgvs [source: hgvs] confidence: high
  Reason: HGVS nomenclature for small variants is NOT APPLICABLE for structural/CNV analysis.
✗ dbnsfp [source: dbnsfp] confidence: high
  Reason: Missense variant meta-resource is NOT APPLICABLE for large-scale structural changes.
✗ sift [source: sift] confidence: high
  Reason: SIFT is for missense amino acid substitutions and is NOT APPLICABLE for CNVs.
✗ polyphen [source: polyphen] confidence: high
  Reason: PolyPhen is for missense amino acid substitutions and is NOT APPLICABLE for CNVs.
✗ cadd [source: cadd] confidence: high
  Reason: CADD is optimized for single nucleotide/small indel pathogenicity and is NOT APPLICABLE here.
✗ spliceai [source: spliceai] confidence: high
  Reason: Predicts splicing effects of small variants and is NOT APPLICABLE for large CNVs.
✗ most_severe [source: most_severe] confidence: high
  Reason: Must be disabled to see all overlapping gene/regulatory consequences rather than just one.
✗ summary [source: summary] confidence: high
  Reason: Must be disabled to provide the detailed annotation required for clinical review.

### 3. Generated VEP Command
```
vep --input_file <input.vcf> --output_file <output.txt> --cache --format vcf --everything --symbol --biotype --regulatory --gnomad_sv --dosage_sensitivity --check_existing --clinvar --phenotypes --numbers --tsl --appris --mane
```
*Note: In the web interface, ensure "Regulatory" and "Dosage Sensitivity" plugins are selected, and use the "Full" output rather than "Summary" or "Most Severe".*
