✓ appris [source: appris, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Helps identify the principal isoforms for clinical interpretation.

  Reason: This is for missense variants; not applicable for large-scale structural/CNV changes.
✓ biotype [source: biotype, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Essential to distinguish between protein-coding genes and non-coding regulatory features.
✓ cadd [source: cadd, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides integrated pathogenicity scores which are useful for clinical assessment.
✓ check_existing [source: check_existing, priority=optional] confidence: medium
  Reason: Required to enable ClinVar and population frequency lookups for co-located variants.
✓ clinvar [source: clinvar, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Critical for clinical interpretation to see if the region/variant has known clinical significance.
✓ core_type [source: core_type, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Ensures the primary annotation set (Ensembl/GENCODE) drives the consequence calculation.
✓ dosage_sensitivity [source: dosage_sensitivity, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Highly relevant for CNVs to determine if a gene is sensitive to copy number changes (e.g., pHaplo).
✓ frequency [source: frequency, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Directly addresses the goal of determining how common variants are in the population.
✓ gnomad_sv [source: gnomad_sv, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Specifically designed to annotate structural variants with gnomAD SV allele frequencies.
✓ mane [source: mane, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides MANE Select/Plus flags to ensure clinical focus on standardized transcripts.
✓ numbers [source: numbers, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides exon/intron numbering which is standard for reporting genomic impacts.
✓ phenotypes [source: phenotypes, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Assists clinical interpretation by linking genes to known phenotypic associations.
✓ regulatory [source: regulatory, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Essential for identifying impacts on non-coding regulatory elements.
✓ symbol [source: symbol, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Makes the output human-readable by including HGNC gene symbols.
✓ tsl [source: tsl, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Adds Transcript Support Level to assess the reliability of the annotated transcripts.

✗ domains [source: domains] confidence: high
  Reason: Primarily for protein domain annotation of small variants, not the focus here.
✗ hgvs [source: hgvs] confidence: high
  Reason: HGVS nomenclature for small variants conflicts with several summary/severity options and is less useful for large CNVs.
✗ most_severe [source: most_severe] confidence: high

✗ spliceai [source: spliceai] confidence: high

  Reason: Predicts missense impact; not applicable for structural variants.
