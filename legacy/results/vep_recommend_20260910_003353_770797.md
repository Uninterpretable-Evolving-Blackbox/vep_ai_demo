### 2. Recommended Options
✓ biotype [source: biotype, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Essential for categorizing the functional nature of the affected regions (e.g., protein_coding vs lincRNA).
✓ check_existing [source: check_existing, priority=optional] confidence: high
  Reason: Necessary to identify known variants and provides the foundation for frequency and ClinVar lookups.
✓ core_type [source: core_type, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Uses the standard Ensembl/GENCODE gene annotation to drive consequence calculations.
✓ dosage_sensitivity [source: dosage_sensitivity, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Critical for CNVs as it annotates genes with dosage sensitivity scores (e.g., pHaplo) from the Collins rCNV catalogue.
✓ frequency [source: frequency, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Directly addresses the goal of incorporating population allele frequency context.
✓ gnomad_sv [source: gnomad_sv, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Specifically designed to annotate structural variants with gnomAD SV allele frequencies.
✓ regulatory [source: regulatory, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Required to assess the impact on regulatory elements as requested.
✓ symbol [source: symbol, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Adds HGNC gene symbols to make the output human-readable and biologically relevant.
✓ tsl [source: tsl, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides Transcript Support Level to help prioritize high-confidence transcripts in coding regions.
✓ numbers [source: numbers, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides exon/intron numbering which is helpful for describing the location of genomic changes.
✗ appris [source: appris] confidence: high
  Reason: While useful, it is a specific transcript annotation not strictly requested over the core requirements.
✗ cadd [source: cadd] confidence: high
  Reason: CADD is primarily optimized for small variants (SNVs/indels) and may not be the primary tool for CNV assessment.
✗ hgvs [source: hgvs] confidence: high
  Reason: HGVS nomenclature is typically used for small variants; it is NOT APPLICABLE for structural variants in this context.
✗ most_severe [source: most_severe] confidence: high
  Reason: Disabling this ensures a full view of all consequences, which is better for CNV/regulatory analysis where multiple hits may occur.
✗ dbnsfp [source: dbnsfp] confidence: high
  Reason: This plugin is for missense variant scoring, which is NOT APPLICABLE to CNVs.
✗ polyphen [source: polyphen] confidence: high
  Reason: This is a tool for predicting amino acid substitution impact, not applicable to structural variants.
✗ sift [source: sift] confidence: high
  Reason: Similar to PolyPhen, this is for small variant missense prediction and is NOT APPLICABLE.
