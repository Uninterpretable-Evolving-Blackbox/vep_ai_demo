### 2. Recommended Options
✓ core_type [source: core_type, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Standard Ensembl/GENCODE annotation is required as the foundation for all consequence calculations.
✓ symbol [source: symbol, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Necessary to provide human-readable HGNC gene symbols for the affected genes.
✓ biotype [source: biotype, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Essential to distinguish between different feature types (e.g., protein_coding vs lincRNA) in regulatory and coding regions.
✓ canonical [source: canonical, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Helps prioritize the most biologically relevant transcript for a given gene.
✓ regulatory [source: regulatory, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Required to assess impacts on promoters, enhancers, and other non-coding regulatory elements.
✓ dosage_sensitivity [source: dosage_sensitivity, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Crucial for CNVs to identify genes where copy-number changes are known to cause disease (e.g., pHaplo).
✓ tsl [source: tsl, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides Transcript Support Level to assess the quality/evidence of the annotated transcripts.
✓ numbers [source: numbers, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides exon/intron numbering which helps localize the boundaries of CNVs/SVs.
✓ gnomad_sv [source: gnomad_sv, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Specifically required to provide population frequency context for structural variants.
✓ check_existing [source: check_existing, priority=optional] confidence: high
  Reason: Enables the lookup of co-located known variants and population frequencies.
✓ clinvar [source: clinvar, priority=optional] confidence: medium
  Reason: Highly recommended to provide clinical context for the identified structural variants.
✗ hgvs [source: hgvs] confidence: high
  Reason: HGVS nomenclature is primarily used for small variants (SNVs/Indels) and is not applicable to CNVs/SVs.
✗ dbnsfp [source: dbnsfp] confidence: high
  Reason: This meta-resource is designed for missense (SNV) variants and is not applicable to structural variants.
✗ sift [source: sift] confidence: high
  Reason: SIFT is a missense pathogenicity predictor for SNVs and does not apply to CNVs.
✗ polyphen [source: polyphen] confidence: high
  Reason: PolyPhen is a missense pathogenicity predictor for SNVs and does not apply to CNVs.
✗ cadd [source: cadd] confidence: high
  Reason: CADD scores are optimized for small variants and are not applicable to structural variants.
✗ most_severe [source: most_severe] confidence: high
  Reason: Disabling this ensures full per-transcript/per-feature detail, which is necessary to understand the exact span of a CNV.
✗ summary [source: summary] confidence: high
  Reason: Disabling this avoids collapsing the detailed regulatory and coding consequences needed for SV interpretation.

### 3. Generated VEP Command
```bash
vep --input_file <input.vcf> --output_file <output.txt> --cache --format vcf --force_overwrite --symbol --biotype --canonical --regulatory --dosage_sensitivity --tsl --numbers --custom gnomad_sv,gnomad_sv.vcf.gz,exact --check_existing --plugin ClinVar
```
*(Note: The `--custom` flag requires a local path to the gnomAD SV VCF file. On the Ensembl Web Interface, these options correspond to the 'Regulatory', 'Dosage Sensitivity', 'TSL', 'Numbers', and 'gnomAD SV' checkboxes.)*
