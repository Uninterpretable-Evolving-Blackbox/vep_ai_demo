  Reason: Essential for the analysis goal of determining how common these structural variations are in the population using gnomAD SV frequencies.

  Reason: Useful for identifying if the structural variants overlap with known variants in other databases.

  Reason: Highly relevant for CNVs (extra or missing DNA) as it annotates genes with known dosage sensitivity (pHaplo) scores.
✓ clinvar [source: clinvar, priority=recommended] confidence: medium
  Reason: Helps with clinical interpretation by flagging if co-located variants have known clinical significance.
✗ hgvs [source: hgvs] confidence: high
  Reason: HGVS nomenclature is primarily designed for short variants (SNPs/indels) and is not applicable to large structural CNVs.
✗ sift [source: sift] confidence: high
  Reason: SIFT predicts the effect of amino acid substitutions; not applicable to structural/non-coding variants.
✗ polyphen [source: polyphen] confidence: high
  Reason: PolyPhen predicts protein structure impact; not applicable to structural/non-coding variants.
✗ cadd [source: cadd] confidence: high
  Reason: CADD scores are optimized for single nucleotide and small indel pathogenicity; not applicable to large CNVs.
✗ most_severe [source: most_severe] confidence: high
  Reason: Disabling this allows the user to see all overlapping regulatory and gene consequences, which is necessary for interpreting complex CNVs.
✗ summary [source: summary] confidence: high
  Reason: Summary mode collapses information; for CNVs, full per-transcript/feature detail is needed to see exact overlap.
