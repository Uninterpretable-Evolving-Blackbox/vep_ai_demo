## User Query
I am starting to set up the pipeline for our human tumor-only whole genome sequencing data from a large cancer cohort, but I am still a bit unsure about how to best capture all the relevant information. We have identified several structural variants and copy number alterations, and my goal is to get a quick top-line summary of their functional consequences.
