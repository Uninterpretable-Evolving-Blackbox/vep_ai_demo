### 2. Recommended Options
✓ core_type [source: core_type, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Standard Ensembl/GENCODE annotation is the foundation for all consequence calculations.
✓ symbol [source: symbol, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Essential for making results human-readable by adding HGNC gene symbols.
✓ biotype [source: biotype, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Helps distinguish if a CNV affects a protein-coding gene, a lincRNA, or another feature.
✓ regulatory [source: regulatory, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Necessary to fulfill the requirement of assessing consequences in regulatory regions.
✓ gnomad_sv [source: gnomad_sv, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Critical for structural variants to determine if the CNV is common in the population or rare.
✓ dosage_sensitivity [source: dosage_sensitivity, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Highly relevant for CNVs (dosage changes) to identify if the affected genes are sensitive to copy number changes.
✓ numbers [source: numbers, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides exon/intron numbering which helps localize the boundaries of the CNV.
✓ tsl [source: tsl, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides Transcript Support Level to help prioritize high-confidence transcripts.
✓ appris [source: appris, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Helps identify functionally important isoforms.
✗ hgvs [source: hgvs] confidence: high
  Reason: HGVS nomenclature is primarily designed for short variants (SNVs/indels) and is not the priority for structural variants.
✗ dbnsfp [source: dbnsfp] confidence: high
  Reason: This plugin is for missense variants; not applicable to large structural variations.
✗ sift [source: sift] confidence: high
  Reason: SIFT predicts the effect of single amino acid substitutions, not the effect of large CNVs.
✗ polyphen [source: polyphen] confidence: high
  Reason: PolyPhen is for missense variants and is not applicable to structural variation.
✗ cadd [source: cadd] confidence: high
  Reason: CADD scores are optimized for short variants; not applicable for CNV-level impact.
✗ most_severe [source: most_severe] confidence: high
  Reason: For structural variants, you need to see all affected features (genes/regulatory elements) rather than a single collapsed consequence.
✗ summary [source: summary] confidence: high
  Reason: Collapses data too much; to understand the functional impact of a CNV, the full set of overlapping features is needed.
✗ coding_only [source: coding_only] confidence: high
  Reason: The user specifically requested focus on both coding and regulatory-noncoding regions.
