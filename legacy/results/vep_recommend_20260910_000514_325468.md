## User Query
I am currently processing germline whole genome sequencing data from a cohort of human pediatric patients presenting with complex neurodevelopmental phenotypes. I need an annotation workflow that can reliably identify the functional impact of both coding SNVs and larger structural variants or CNVs, ensuring that any protein-altering changes are cross-referenced with MANE Select transcripts for diagnostic certainty. Additionally, I need to capture potential disruptions in regulatory and non-coding regions to assess their impact on gene expression; the primary objective is to produce a streamlined output of consequence calls and population allele frequencies to support my clinical interpretation of pathogenicity.
