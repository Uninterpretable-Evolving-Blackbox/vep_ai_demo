  Reason: Provides gnomAD structural-variant allele frequencies, which is the primary requirement to assess population frequency of CNVs.

✓ dosage_sensitivity [source: dosage_sensitivity, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Highly relevant for germline CNVs (deletions/duplications) as it annotates gene dosage sensitivity (e.g., pHaplo).

✓ check_existing [source: check_existing, priority=optional] confidence: medium
  Reason: Allows the detection of known variants co-located with the input to provide additional context.

✗ hgvs [source: hgvs] confidence: high
  Reason: HGVS nomenclature is typically used for small variants (SNVs/indels) and is not applicable for structural variants in this context.

✗ sift [source: sift] confidence: high
  Reason: SIFT predicts the effect of missense amino acid substitutions, which does not apply to structural variants.

✗ polyphen [source: polyphen] confidence: high
  Reason: PolyPhen predicts the impact of missense substitutions, which is not applicable to CNVs.

✗ cadd [source: cadd] confidence: high
  Reason: CADD scores are designed for single nucleotide and small indel variants, not structural variants.

✗ most_severe [source: most_severe] confidence: high
  Reason: For structural variants, you need the full context of all overlapped genes and biotypes rather than a single collapsed consequence.

✗ summary [source: summary] confidence: high
  Reason: A summary output would hide the detailed gene and frequency information required for clinical review.
