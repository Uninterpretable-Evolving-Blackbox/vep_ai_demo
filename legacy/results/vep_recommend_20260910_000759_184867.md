✓ appris [source: appris, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Helps identify the principal isoform for clinical interpretation.
✓ biotype [source: biotype, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Essential to distinguish between protein-coding genes and regulatory/non-coding features.

  Reason: Provides a combined deleteriousness score helpful for clinical pathogenicity assessment.
✓ check_existing [source: check_existing, priority=optional] confidence: high
  Reason: Necessary to link variants to known IDs and population frequency data.
✓ clinvar [source: clinvar, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides clinical significance (e.g., pathogenic) for clinical interpretation.
✓ core_type [source: core_type, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Required to drive the consequence calculation using Ensembl/GENCODE annotations.
✓ dosage_sensitivity [source: dosage_sensitivity, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Crucial for interpreting CNVs (duplications/deletions) via pHaplo scores.
✓ gnomad_sv [source: gnomad_sv, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides allele frequencies specifically for structural variants in the gnomAD database.
✓ regulatory [source: regulatory, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Required to assess the impact of structural changes on regulatory regions.
✓ symbol [source: symbol, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Adds HGNC gene symbols to make results human-readable for clinical reports.
✓ tsl [source: tsl, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Adds Transcript Support Level to evaluate the reliability of the transcripts being affected.
✓ phenotypes [source: phenotypes, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Links identified gene disruptions to known phenotypes for clinical context.
✗ af_gnomade [source: af_gnomade, priority=NOT APPLICABLE] confidence: high
  Reason: This is exome-only data; gnomAD SV is more appropriate for structural variants.
✗ hgvs [source: hgvs, priority=NOT APPLICABLE] confidence: high
  Reason: HGVS nomenclature is primarily for small variants (SNVs/indels), not structural variants.

  Reason: Conflicts with the ability to see multiple consequence terms across coding and regulatory regions.
✗ spliceai [source: spliceai, priority=NOT APPLICABLE] confidence: high
  Reason: Specifically for small-scale splice-site alterations, not structural/CNV changes.

