## User Query
I am reviewing human germline whole genome sequencing for a patient with suspected large-scale chromosomal changes. I need to quickly identify the impact on genes and regulatory regions, as well as how common these variants are in the general population.

- **region_focus**: coding, regulatory-noncoding (Explicitly stated "impact on genes and regulatory regions")
- **analysis_goal**: clinical-interpretation, population-frequency (Indicated by "identify the impact" and "how common... in the general population")

### 2. Recommended Options
✓ core_type [source: core_type, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Essential to use the Ensembl/GENCODE core annotation to drive consequence calculations.
✓ symbol [source: symbol, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Adds HGNC gene symbols to make results more human-interpretable.
✓ biotype [source: biotype, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Necessary to distinguish if the structural change affects protein-coding genes, lincRNA, etc.
✓ regulatory [source: regulatory, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Explicitly requested to identify impact on regulatory regions (promoters, enhancers).
✓ gnomad_sv [source: gnomad_sv, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Specifically required to assess population frequency for structural variants using gnomAD SV data.
✓ dosage_sensitivity [source: dosage_sensitivity, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Highly relevant for CNVs to identify if the affected genes are sensitive to dosage changes (e.g., pHaplo).
✓ check_existing [source: check_existing, priority=optional] confidence: medium
  Reason: Recommended to see if the structural variant is already cataloged in known databases.
✓ clinvar [source: clinvar, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Crucial for clinical interpretation to see if the region/variant has known clinical significance.
✓ phenotypes [source: phenotypes, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Helps clinical interpretation by linking affected genes to known phenotypes.
✓ numbers [source: numbers, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides exon/intron numbering which helps in localizing the impact of the CNV.
✓ tsl [source: tsl, priority=recommended] confidence: medium
  Reason: Helps prioritize transcripts based on their transcript support level.
✓ appris [source: appris, priority=recommended] confidence: medium
  Reason: Helps identify functionally important isoforms, which is critical when a CNV disrupts specific transcripts.
✗ mane [source: mane] confidence: high
  Reason: While useful for coding variants, for large-scale CNVs, focusing on all overlapping transcripts is often more informative than just the MANE transcript.
✗ sift [source: sift] confidence: high
  Reason: Missense predictors are not applicable to structural variants/CNVs.
✗ polyphen [source: polyphen] confidence: high
  Reason: Missense predictors are not applicable to structural variants/CNVs.
✗ cadd [source: cadd] confidence: high
  Reason: Missense predictors/scores are not applicable to structural variants/CNVs.
✗ most_severe [source: most_severe] confidence: high
  Reason: For CNVs, you need to see all affected genes and transcripts, not just a single collapsed consequence.
✗ summary [source: summary] confidence: high
  Reason: A summary view hides the detailed gene/regulatory impact required for clinical review.

### 3. Generated VEP Command
```
vep --input_file <input.vcf> --output_file <output.txt> --cache --format vcf --plugin DosageSensitivity --plugin Phenotypes --plugin gnomad_sv --check_existing --clinvar --regulatory --symbol --biotype --tsl --appris --numbers
```
*Note: On the Ensembl Web Interface, ensure you select 'Human' as the species and check the boxes for 'Regulatory', 'Gene Symbol', 'Biotype', and the specific plugins mentioned.*
