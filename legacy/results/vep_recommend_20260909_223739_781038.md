## User Query
I am reviewing human germline whole genome sequencing for a patient with suspected large-scale chromosomal changes. I need to quickly identify the impact on genes and regulatory regions, as well as how common these variants are in the general population.

## Recommendation
### 1. Detected Scenario
- **species**: human (specified "human germline")
- **origin**: germline (specified "germline whole genome sequencing")
- **variant_size_class**: structural-CNV (specified "large-scale chromosomal changes")
- **region_focus**: coding, regulatory-noncoding (specified "impact on genes and regulatory regions")
- **analysis_goal**: clinical-interpretation, population-frequency (specified "identify the impact" and "how common these variants are in the general population")

### 2. Recommended Options
✓ appris [source: appris, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Helps identify functionally important isoforms, which is critical for clinical interpretation of gene disruptions.
✓ biotype [source: biotype, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Essential for distinguishing between protein-coding genes and other features like lincRNA or pseudogenes in the affected regions.
✓ cadd [source: cadd, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides a combined score for deleteriousness, assisting in clinical interpretation of the variants.
✓ check_existing [source: check_existing, priority=optional] confidence: high
  Reason: Required to link input variants to known IDs like rsIDs or ClinVar entries.
✓ clinvar [source: clinvar, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides essential clinical significance data (e.g., pathogenic/benign) for interpreting findings.
✓ core_type [source: core_type, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Standard requirement to define the transcript annotation set driving the consequences.
✓ dosage_sensitivity [source: dosage_sensitivity, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Crucial for structural variants/CNVs to determine if the gene is sensitive to changes in copy number (e.g., pHaplo).
✓ frequency [source: frequency, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Enables filtering/reporting based on population allele frequencies to meet the user's goal.
✓ gnomad_sv [source: gnomad_sv, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Specifically provides population frequency data for structural variants, which is the user's primary variant class.
✓ mane [source: mane, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Ensures clinical certainty by flagging transcripts that match NCBI/Ensembl standards.
✓ numbers [source: numbers, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides exon/intron numbering, helping to locate the exact position of the chromosomal change within a gene.
✓ phenotypes [source: phenotypes, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Links variants to known clinical phenotypes to assist in diagnostic interpretation.
✓ regulatory [source: regulatory, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Necessary to identify impacts on the non-coding/regulatory regions requested by the user.
✓ symbol [source: symbol, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Adds HGNC gene symbols for human-readable and clinically relevant output.
✓ tsl [source: tsl, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Provides Transcript Support Level to help assess the reliability of the annotated transcripts.
✗ hgvs [source: hgvs] confidence: high
  Reason: HGVS nomenclature is primarily for small variants (SNVs/indels) and is not applicable to structural-CNV analysis.
✗ most_severe [source: most_severe] confidence: high
  Reason: This flag conflicts with several recommended clinical annotation options like --symbol and --tsl.
✗ spliceai [source: spliceai] confidence: high
  Reason: This tool is optimized for predicting the impact of small-scale splice-site variants rather than large-scale CNVs.

### 3. Generated VEP Command
```
vep --input_file <input.vcf> --output_file <output.txt> --cache --plugin DosageSensitivity --plugin gnomad_sv --plugin Phenotypes --plugin CADD --plugin ClinVar --plugin mane --plugin appris --appris --biotype --symbol --tsl --mane --regulatory --frequency --check_existing --numbers
```
*(Note: In the web interface, ensure you select the "Structural Variant" allele frequency options and enable the specific plugins mentioned for CNV analysis.)*
