  Reason: Using the default ENsembl/GENCODE core annotation is standard and appropriate for a general analysis setting.
✓ biotype [source: none, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Since the analysis focuses on both genes and regulatory regions, including the biotype helps characterize the functional category of the affected regions (e.g., protein_coding, lincRNA).
✓ symbol [source: none, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: To easily identify the genes affected by structural changes, adding the gene symbol is highly beneficial for interpretation.
✓ regulatory [source: none, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: The user explicitly wants to identify the impact on "regulatory regions," making this plugin essential.
✓ check_existing [source: none, priority=optional] confidence: high
  Reason: Checking for known co-located variants (like common structural variants or genes) helps contextually place the observed changes within known human biology.
✓ frequency [source: none, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: The goal requires knowing how "common these variants are in the general population," which requires frequency annotations.
✓ numbers [source: none, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: When dealing with structural variants, knowing the affected exon and intron numbering provides critical structural context beyond simple gene overlap.
✓ gnomad_sv [source: none, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Since the input is large-scale structural variants (CNVs), using the gnomAD structural variant annotation is the most direct way to assess population frequency for these specific types of changes.
✗ hgvs [source: none] confidence: medium
  Reason: While useful, HGVS nomenclature is often challenging to interpret correctly for large structural variations compared to using the gene name and SV boundaries directly.
✗ most_severe [source: none] confidence: high
  Reason: Structural variations affect large genomic regions and multiple transcripts/features. Using `--most_severe` would collapse the critical information about all overlapping regulatory and coding regions into a single, potentially misleading, result.
✗ af_gnomade [source: none] confidence: medium
  Reason: `--gnomad_sv` is a more specific and appropriate resource for frequency assessment of structural variants compared to the general exome/genome population frequencies.
✗ af_gnomadg [source: none] confidence: medium
  Reason: Similar to above, `--gnomad_sv` is the specialized tool needed for structural variants, making the general `--gnomad_sv` preferred.
✗ sift [source: none] confidence: high
  Reason: SIFT predicts amino acid changes (missense). Structural CNVs may span large non-coding or multi-exon regions, making missense prediction irrelevant or inapplicable for the primary interpretation goal.
✗ polyphen [source: none] confidence: high
  Reason: PolyPhen predicts amino acid changes, which are not the primary focus when analyzing large structural changes; the focus is on region overlap and frequency.
✗ cadd [source: none] confidence: high
  Reason: CADD scoring is generally for point mutations and single-nucleotide changes, making it inappropriate for comprehensive structural variant analysis.
