- **variant_size_class**: small (SNVs and indels)
- **region_focus**: regulatory-noncoding (impact on regulatory/non-coding elements)
- **analysis_goal**: basic-consequence (quickly identify impact)

### 2. Recommended Options
✓ core_type [source: core_type, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Essential to define the transcript/gene annotation set driving the consequence calculation.
✓ biotype [source: biotype, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: Necessary to distinguish between protein-coding, lincRNA, and other non-coding elements in the regulatory context.
✓ regulatory [source: regulatory, priority=recommended] confidence: high
  Reason: The primary requirement for the user is to find overlaps with regulatory regions (promoters, enhancers, etc.).
✓ canonical [source: canonical, priority=recommended] confidence: medium
  Reason: Helps prioritize the most biologically relevant transcript/feature when multiple overlaps occur.
✓ check_existing [source: check_existing, priority=optional] confidence: medium
  Reason: Recommended for somatic studies to identify if the variants have been previously catalogued in zebrafish variation databases.
