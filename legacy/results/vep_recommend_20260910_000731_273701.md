  Reason: Critical for CNVs (duplications/deletions) to determine if the gene is sensitive to copy number changes (e.g., haploinsufficiency).

  Reason: Aids clinical interpretation by linking genes to known phenotypic associations.

  Reason: Provides a combined deleteriousness score to assist in clinical assessment of variant impact.

  Reason: Provides gnomAD LOEUF scores to help judge if a gene is constrained by selection, useful for interpreting rare variants.

  Reason: Directly supports clinical interpretation by searching for medical literature evidence.
